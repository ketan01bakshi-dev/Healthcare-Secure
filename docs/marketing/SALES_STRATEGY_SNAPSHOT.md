# Sales & Marketing Strategy — Aarogya One Connect

**Target Market:** Small to Medium Clinics and Polyclinics in India.

## 1. Positioning Strategy
Aarogya One Connect is positioned as a **"Professional OPD Operating System"** at the **lower end of each market price band**, with **premium workflow value** bundled in.

### Price positioning (Aug 2026)
| Plan | Price | Band position |
|------|-------|---------------|
| Starter | ₹1,999/mo · ₹19,990/yr | Lower solo OPD band (vs Eka ₹1,562/mo, Clinicea ₹1,999/doc) |
| Clinic | ₹5,499/mo · ₹54,990/yr | Lower polyclinic band (~45% below Eka Clinic Pro) |
| Network | from ₹12,999/mo | Lower chain entry vs typical HMS |

### Key Differentiators:
- **Value narrative:** "Premium OPD workflow at budget-tier pricing."
- **Mobility:** Doctor on phone; front desk on PC; lab and nurse on their own desks.
- **Privacy:** HMAC Blind Identity — searchable phone numbers never stored.
- **Specialization:** Gynecology depth (obstetric cards, GA trends, ANC alerts).
- **No hidden fees:** Flat clinic price; no marketplace commission; no per-appointment surcharge.

### vs Competitors (see `COMPETITOR_BENCHMARKING.md`)
- **HealthPlix (2014):** Scale & ABHA — we win on flat clinic price + 4 desks + gynae.
- **Eka Care (2020):** WhatsApp engagement — we win on predictable price, no credit-metered msgs.
- **Practo Ray (2008):** Patient discovery — we win on zero commission, data ownership.
- **Clinicea (2012):** Custom EMR — we win on Voice Rx + lower 5-doc TCO.

## 3. Doctor Value Talking Points
1. **15–20 min/day saved** with Voice Rx (60–80 hrs/year).
2. **Flat bill** — doesn't rise when OPD gets busier (unlike Practo marketplace).
3. **4 desks included** — not per-user billing like DocEngage.
4. **UPI QR on patient card** — faster collections, less reconciliation.
5. **14-day risk-free pilot** — try on real patients before committing.
