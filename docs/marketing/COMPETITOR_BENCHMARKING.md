# Competitor Benchmarking — Aarogya One Connect (Aug 2026)

**Purpose:** Market positioning, pricing validation, and sales narrative for Indian OPD clinic software.

**Pricing stance:** Aarogya is positioned at the **lower end of each category band** while delivering **premium workflow value** (Voice Rx, 4-role desks, gynae depth, privacy architecture).

## 2. Competitor Profiles

### 2.1 HealthPlix
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Founded** | 2014 (Bengaluru) |
| **Product age** | ~12 years; mature EMR network |
| **Scale** | 12,000+ doctors; $22M+ Series C |
| **Pricing** | ₹11,999–17,999/**doctor/year** (~₹999–1,500/mo per doctor) |
| **Model** | Per-doctor annual subscription |

**Doctor reviews — positive**
- Fast digital prescriptions; 14+ regional Rx languages
- Strong for solo consultants; long-tenured users (9+ years cited)
- AI scribe (H.A.L.O) and clinical decision support on higher tiers
- ABHA/ABDM integration built in

**Doctor reviews — negative**
- Pricing varies by product line; not always transparent upfront
- Per-doctor billing grows as clinic adds practitioners
- Less emphasis on front-desk / reception / lab role separation
- Hospital/IPD depth limited on clinic tiers

**Position vs Aarogya:** HealthPlix wins on **brand scale & ABDM**. Aarogya wins on **flat clinic pricing, 4-desk OPD OS, gynae workflows, HMAC privacy** — at comparable solo cost when staff seats are included.

---

### 2.2 Eka Care (eka.doc)
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Founded** | Dec 2020 (Bengaluru) |
| **Product age** | ~5 years; fast-growing, VC-backed ($19.5M) |
| **Scale** | 5,000+ doctors on EMR; ABDM-approved ABHA platform |
| **Pricing** | Doc Plus ₹16,999/yr · Doc Pro ₹18,749/yr · Clinic Pro ₹1,00,000/yr (5 doc + 15 staff) |
| **Model** | Annual bundles + paid add-ons (WhatsApp credits, pharmacy, success manager) |

**Doctor reviews — positive**
- Smooth UI; WhatsApp prescription delivery
- Google My Business / online presence tools
- On-site onboarding praised by hospital chains
- AI suggestions for symptoms, diagnoses, medicines

**Doctor reviews — negative**
- Mixed support quality (SoftwareAdvice: 1.0 rating from sampled reviews)
- “Pathetic service”, unresponsive customer care cited
- Hidden-style costs: ₹1/WhatsApp msg, teleconsult 50p/min after 200 min, storage ₹1,200/20GB
- Clinic Pro annual-only; less flexible for monthly budgeting

**Position vs Aarogya:** Eka wins on **patient engagement & ABDM go-live**. Aarogya wins on **predictable flat monthly price, Voice Rx included, no credit-metered WhatsApp for core OPD, 40%+ cheaper at polyclinic scale**.

---

### 2.3 Practo Ray
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Founded** | 2008 (Ray launched 2008–2009) |
| **Product age** | ~17 years; largest patient marketplace in India |
| **Scale** | 1 lakh+ doctor partners; 20+ countries |
| **Pricing** | ₹999–3,999/mo tiers + **marketplace commissions** (effective ₹3,500–5,000+/mo busy clinics) |
| **Model** | SaaS + patient discovery marketplace + consultation commission |

**Doctor reviews — positive**
- Patient discovery for new practices
- Established brand; patients already search on Practo
- Appointments, EMR, billing in one ecosystem
- Teleconsult infrastructure

**Doctor reviews — negative**
- Per-appointment fees on marketplace bookings (₹1,500–3,000/mo at 30–50 patients/day)
- Doctors feel **commoditised** in marketplace; pay for visibility (Practo Prime/Reach)
- Play Store: “just want money”, profile verification delays, support unresponsive
- Complex pricing; bill rises as practice grows
- Practo-branded patient journey — not white-label

**Position vs Aarogya:** Practo wins on **new patient acquisition**. Aarogya wins on **your patients, your data, zero commission, flat bill** — ideal for established clinics that don't need marketplace leads.

---

### 2.4 Clinicea
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Founded** | 2012 (team in healthcare since 2004) |
| **Product age** | ~14 years; global deployments (6 continents) |
| **Pricing** | ₹1,999–3,999/**practitioner/mo** (Starter/Pro/Enterprise) |
| **Model** | Per-practitioner; annual billing typical |

**Doctor reviews — positive**
- Custom EMR templates; “feels like paper” flexibility
- Excellent support responsiveness (multiple 6-year+ users)
- Inventory, pharmacy, billing, packages in one system
- Zero bug issues cited by long-term hospital users

**Doctor reviews — negative**
- Per-doctor pricing expensive at polyclinic scale (5 docs ≈ ₹10,000–15,000/mo)
- Pricing may not suit very small clinics
- WhatsApp two-way messaging gaps; referral acknowledgement to referring doctor missing
- Fewer pre-built integrations vs newer platforms

**Position vs Aarogya:** Clinicea wins on **custom EMR depth & pharmacy/inventory**. Aarogya wins on **Voice Rx, flat clinic price, gynae specialty, lower TCO for 3–5 doctor setups**.

## 3. Aarogya One Connect — Self Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Product age** | 2025–2026 (modern OPD OS; v1.60+) |
| **Category** | Professional OPD Operating System (not legacy EMR) |
| **Pricing (Aug 2026)** | Starter **₹1,999/mo** · Clinic **₹5,499/mo** · Network **from ₹12,999/mo** |
| **Model** | Flat per-clinic; no marketplace commission; no per-appointment fees |

**Strengths to sell**
1. **Voice-to-Rx** — dictate in Hindi/English; structured signed PDF in seconds
2. **4-role desks** — Doctor, Staff, Reception, Lab on separate devices
3. **Gynae / obstetric depth** — ANC alerts, GA trends, consult packs
4. **HMAC Blind Identity** — searchable phone numbers never stored
5. **Razorpay UPI QR** — instant identifiable payments on patient card
6. **HL7 lab ingest** — diagnostic desk integration
7. **Forward case / referral handoff** — multi-doctor collaboration
8. **Flat pricing** — bill doesn't rise when OPD gets busier

**Honest gaps (don't oversell)**
- No patient marketplace (Practo)
- ABDM care-context not fully live yet
- No pharmacy/inventory module (Clinicea/MocDoc)
- No IPD/ward (by design — OPD focus)
- Newer brand vs 12–17 year incumbents

## 5. Doctor Value Narrative (sales copy)

### The pitch in one line
> **"Premium OPD workflow at budget-tier pricing — because your time is worth more than your software bill."**

### Value math for doctors

| Value lever | Doctor benefit | Estimated impact |
|-------------|----------------|------------------|
| **Voice Rx** | 15–20 min saved per doctor per day on typing | ~60–80 hrs/year recovered |
| **UPI QR on patient card** | Faster fee collection; less front-desk chasing | Reduced leakage & reconciliation time |
| **4 desks aligned** | Reception, nurse, lab, doctor see same patient in real time | Fewer walk-out errors & duplicate entry |
| **Flat pricing** | No surprise bill when OPD volume grows | Predictable monthly budgeting |
| **Gynae workflows** | ANC alerts, GA trends built-in | Less cognitive load in high-volume ANC clinics |
| **Privacy architecture** | DPDP-aligned; patient trust | Lower compliance anxiety |

### Category positioning statements

**vs HealthPlix / Eka (solo):**
> "Same monthly band — but we include 3 staff seats, Voice Rx, and four OPD desks. No annual lock-in required on Starter."

**vs Practo Ray:**
> "No commission on your patients. You keep 100% of consultation fees. Our bill stays flat when you get busier."

**vs Clinicea (polyclinic):**
> "Half the cost at 5 doctors — with AI prescription dictation included, not sold separately."

**vs MocDoc / hospital HIS:**
> "You don't need IPD complexity. You need OPD speed. Go live in days, not weeks."
