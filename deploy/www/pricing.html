Frequently Asked Questions

Who controls our patient data?
You do. Your clinic is the Data Fiduciary. Aarogya One Connect acts as a Data Processor. We provide the tools and security; you retain legal ownership and control over the records.
Is there a setup or implementation fee?
Self-onboarding for Starter/Clinic plans is free. Implementation fees apply for Network plans or clinics requiring custom on-site training and data migration.
How does the 14-day pilot work?
We provide a fully functional private tenant for your clinic. You can use it on real patients for two weeks. If you choose not to continue, you can export your data and there is no obligation.
Do you support GST billing?
Yes. All professional invoices include GST details for your accounting and input tax credits.
