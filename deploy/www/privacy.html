Privacy

Staff clinic software — not a consumer app.

Last updated: August 2026

Aarogya One Connect is used by clinic staff to record visits, vitals, lab results, and prescriptions. It is not a patient portal and does not sell data to advertisers.

What staff enter

Sign-in identifiers and PIN verification (PINs are stored hashed). Patient name, mobile, or MRN are used to compute a blind identifier. Clinical notes, vitals, labs, prescription fields, and uploaded documents stay on the clinic’s configured server.

Where it lives

Data is stored on the clinic operator’s API (cloud VPS or clinic server), not on this marketing site. The public pages at www.aarogyaoneconnect.in do not collect clinical records.

Sharing

Staff may print or share a prescription PDF using the device share sheet when they choose to. There is no advertising SDK.

Controller

The clinic that signs in is the data controller for its patients under applicable law (including India’s DPDP Act). This product site does not replace that clinic’s own notice to patients.
