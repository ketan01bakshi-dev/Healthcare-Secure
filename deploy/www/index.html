Clinic software · India

The OPD desk that travels with the doctor — and stays on the front desk PC.

One product for gynecology and GP clinics: lock a patient, take vitals, dictate a prescription, show a UPI QR, send the lab order. Staff phones on 4G. Reception on a browser. Lab on its own desk.

Open clinic desk See the workflow

Staff-only. Not a patient portal. Clinics sign in with their own clinic name and roles.

What a visit looks like

  1. Pick the patient Front desk or doctor opens Patient Info → All patients (name, mobile, or MRN). Waiting list works the same way.
  2. Do the work of that desk Staff enter vitals. Doctor lands on Visit after lock — voice to a signed Rx PDF, case brief, recommended labs. Lab sees those ticks on another phone.
  3. Close the loop Charges and UPI QR on Patient Info. Records keep the timeline. Analytics on More — counts, not a hospital warehouse.

Voice to signed Rx

Dictate in the Visit tab. Speech-to-text and a structured parse become a letterhead PDF the doctor reviews and signs. Print or share from the phone.

Front-desk money

Charge ledger on Patient Info. Optional Razorpay UPI QR so the patient pays without a WhatsApp screenshot of someone else’s UPI app.

Lab without the Rx pad

Lab Desk is Today, Labs, and More — results and PDFs only. Visit ticks persist on the patient so a second device sees the order list.

Four desks, one clinic

Role Sees Does not

Staff Patient Info, Vitals, Records, More Sign prescriptions

Lab Today, Labs, More Billing, Visit

Identity that does not leave a phone column

Staff still type a name, mobile, or MRN. The API stores a keyed hash (blind ID), not a searchable phone book. Clinics are isolated by clinic ID on one shared cloud API.

This is a staff tool. There is no advertising SDK and no consumer “my reports” login.
